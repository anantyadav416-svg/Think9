The Core Bottleneck

Linear Scaling Bottlenecks

  • • Fragmented vendor interactions and duplicate sourcing across multiple independent brand teams.
  • • Slow reactive market research failing to capture fast-moving Indian consumer shifts.
  • • High creative latency in producing performance ad variations and packaging assets.
The Architectural Pivot

Unified Intelligence Backbone

  • • Central data aggregation layer pulling unstructured PDFs, vendor quotes, and review streams.
  • • Domain-specific autonomous agent nodes (Sourcing, Intelligence, Content) with strict guardrails.
  • • Human-in-the-loop validation dashboards for brand identity and quality assurance.
